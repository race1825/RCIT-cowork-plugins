# Proposals — Race-Conz IT Solutions

## Purpose
Use this file when writing project proposals, client bids, letters of intent, or technical offers for any Race-Conz IT Solutions engagement.

---

## Proposal Tone
- **Formal but human** — Government and LGU readers expect structure; cooperative and SME readers appreciate clarity
- **Confidence without arrogance** — Cite credentials, not superlatives
- **Problem-first** — Always open by naming the client's pain point before offering the solution

---

## Standard Proposal Structure

### 1. Cover / Title Block
```
PROJECT PROPOSAL
[Service / Project Title]
Submitted to: [Client Name / Organization]
Submitted by: Race-Conz IT Solutions
              Horace B. Briones, Proprietor & Project Manager
Date: [Date]
```

### 2. Executive Summary (100–150 words)
- What Race-Conz is proposing
- Why this client needs it now
- The expected outcome / benefit
- One-line credibility statement (18+ years, Sophos/Microsoft partner, Bicol-based)

### 3. Project Background / Problem Statement
- Describe the current situation or challenge the client is facing
- Reference the client's sector where possible (LGU infrastructure gaps, cooperative data security, hotel network reliability)
- Keep factual; avoid dramatizing

### 4. Proposed Solution
- Describe the specific services/systems to be delivered
- Use numbered phases if multi-stage (Phase 1: Assessment, Phase 2: Deployment, etc.)
- Name actual technologies, brands, and standards (Sophos XGS firewall, CAT6 cabling, Microsoft 365, etc.)
- Mention Sophos/Microsoft partnership where relevant to the solution

### 5. Scope of Work
Use a simple table or bulleted list:
- Inclusions (what Race-Conz will deliver)
- Exclusions (what is not covered — avoids disputes later)
- Site requirements (what the client must provide)

### 6. Project Timeline
Simple table: Phase | Activity | Duration | Responsible Party

### 7. Company Profile (brief)
> Race-Conz IT Solutions is a Bicol-based IT services firm with over 18 years of experience. As a Sophos and Microsoft partner, we specialize in cybersecurity, network infrastructure, systems integration, and cloud services. We have served Local Government Units, cooperatives, hotels, resorts, and private businesses across the Bicol region — always with a hands-on approach from planning through deployment.

### 8. Pricing / Investment Summary
- Present as an "investment" not a "cost"
- Use a clean table: Item | Description | Qty | Unit Price | Total
- Always include a subtotal, VAT line (if applicable), and grand total
- Include payment terms (e.g., 50% downpayment, 50% upon completion)

### 9. Terms & Conditions (brief)
- Validity of proposal (e.g., "This proposal is valid for 30 days")
- Warranty terms
- Support/maintenance terms if applicable

### 10. Closing & Signature Block
> We look forward to partnering with [Client Name] in building a more secure and connected [organization type]. For questions or to proceed, please contact us at [contact info].

```
Prepared by:
Horace B. Briones
Proprietor & Project Manager
Race-Conz IT Solutions
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]
```

---

## Proposal Tips
- **LGU / Government proposals**: Include BAC (Bids and Awards Committee) formatting if for public bidding. Use formal Filipino government terminology where required.
- **Cooperative proposals**: Emphasize cost-efficiency, ROI, and compliance with cooperative standards.
- **Hotel / Resort proposals**: Lead with guest experience and uptime reliability benefits.
- **SME proposals**: Keep short (2–3 pages max). Focus on simplicity and speed of delivery.
