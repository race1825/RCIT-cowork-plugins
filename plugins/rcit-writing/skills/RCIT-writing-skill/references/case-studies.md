# Case Studies & Project Write-Ups — Race-Conz IT Solutions

## Purpose
Use this file when writing project case studies, success stories, or project summaries that showcase Race-Conz IT Solutions' completed work.

---

## Case Study Structure

### Title
`How Race-Conz [Solved X] for [Client Type] in Bicol`
or
`[Client Type] Achieves [Outcome] with Race-Conz IT Solutions`

> Do not name specific clients without permission. Use descriptive labels like "a Bicol cooperative", "a mid-sized hotel in Legazpi", "a Local Government Unit in Camarines Sur."

### At a Glance (sidebar or box)
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Client | [Type, e.g., "Regional Cooperative"] |
| Location | Bicol Region, Philippines |
| Service Delivered | [e.g., Network Infrastructure Rehabilitation] |
| Timeline | [e.g., 3 weeks] |
| Key Outcome | [e.g., 99.9% network uptime achieved] |

### The Challenge
2–3 paragraphs describing:
- Who the client is and their context
- The specific IT problem or gap they were facing
- Why the situation was urgent or important

### The Approach
How Race-Conz assessed the situation and designed the solution:
- Discovery / site assessment
- Solution design
- Technologies / vendors selected (name them: Sophos, CAT6, Microsoft 365, etc.)

### The Execution
What Race-Conz actually did — step by step in plain language:
- Phase 1: [Activity]
- Phase 2: [Activity]
- Race's personal involvement where notable

### The Results
Quantify where possible:
- "Reduced network downtime by [X]%"
- "Secured [X] endpoints across [Y] branches"
- "Completed within [X]-week timeline and within budget"
- "Staff now able to access systems remotely via VPN"

Include a short client quote if available (even paraphrased/anonymized with permission).

### Why It Matters
1–2 sentences on the broader significance — for similar clients reading this.

### CTA
> "Facing similar challenges? Race-Conz IT Solutions can assess and solve your infrastructure needs. [Contact us for a free consultation →]"

---

## Tips for Writing Case Studies

- **Be specific** — "Installed a Sophos XGS firewall and reconfigured 3 VLANs" beats "upgraded their security"
- **Tell a story** — Problem → tension → resolution; don't just list activities
- **Protect client privacy** — Always anonymize unless written consent obtained
- **Use outcomes, not features** — "Staff can now work securely from home" beats "VPN was deployed"
- **Keep it to 500–800 words** for web use; longer for formal capability statements or bid submissions
